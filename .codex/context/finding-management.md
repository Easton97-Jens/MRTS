# MRTS Finding Management Delta

Use IDs beginning with `FND-MRTS-` for MRTS-owned findings.

Every finding requires concrete evidence, an attacker/error-controlled source or violated contract, the relevant control/sink, impact, preconditions, a legitimate control, strongest counterevidence, validation method, confidence, and owning repository.

A suspicious pattern is not automatically a validated vulnerability. Cross-repository causes are reported to the owner and are not patched from an MRTS-only task.
